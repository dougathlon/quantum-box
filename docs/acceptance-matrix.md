## Future audience research — non-gating

The earlier human-playtest rows are retained as research questions, not as
conditions for this build, integration goal, or release handoff:

- Can an unfamiliar player complete the Story stages within a bounded number of
  attempts without the CPUs feeling inert or impossible?
- Does each stripped Arcade loop sustain attention, and can a player state what
  they were trying to improve?
- Do repeated attempts produce more control or a more precise hypothesis about
  a hidden rule, residual field, relation, or topology change?
- Can a player accurately distinguish provider evidence, local decoding,
  classical runtime, and fiction after the Story presentation and terminal
  sequence?

These observations may motivate later design revisions. Their absence does not
convert deterministic, provenance, accessibility, or release checks into a
failure.
